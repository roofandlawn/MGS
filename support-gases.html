Nitrogen NF + Instrument Air

Keep support gases separate from patient-breathing medical gases.

This view teaches the field relationship from source to equipment outlet for nitrogen NF and instrument air. The emphasis is source recognition, higher-pressure hazards, pressure control, warning-system relationships, distribution, identification, and verification — not compressor repair or cylinder-handling technique.

Field distinction

Medical support gas is not a breathing-gas service.

NFPA 99 treats nitrogen NF and instrument air as medical support gases used primarily to power equipment used in patient-care procedures. Confirm the designed service pressure and equipment requirements instead of assuming the 50–55 psig range used by many patient gases.
