What to notice

Category 1 vacuum is built around continued availability.

The 2024 source requirements address multiple pumps, isolation, filtration, receiver serviceability, controls, and arrangement so a single fault or service activity does not unnecessarily remove the entire source.

Conceptual learning diagram only. Flow direction is shown from the facility/use side toward the vacuum producer and exhaust. Actual receiver, filter, pump, valve, control, and piping arrangements vary by listed equipment technology and project.
