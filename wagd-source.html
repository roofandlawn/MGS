What to notice

WAGD is its own service.

A WAGD system can use the medical-surgical vacuum source under specific conditions, a dedicated producer, or a venturi source. The app keeps WAGD identification, alarms, source logic, and terminal relationships separate so users do not treat it as simply another vacuum inlet.

Conceptual learning diagram only. The path shown represents a dedicated central producer. Shared medical-surgical vacuum and venturi arrangements differ. Actual valves, controls, alarm points, recovery/destruction equipment, and exhaust arrangements vary by project and listed equipment.
