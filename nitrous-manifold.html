Field identity

Nitrous oxide is its own medical-gas service.

NFPA 99-2024 Table 5.1.11 identifies N₂O with blue/white service colors and a standard 50–55 psi gauge pressure range. Keep gas identity, labeling, outlet service, alarms, and verification tied to the N₂O system rather than treating it as interchangeable with oxygen or WAGD.
